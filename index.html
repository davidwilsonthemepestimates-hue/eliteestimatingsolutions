Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our construction estimating services. Last updated October 2026.

What does Elite Estimating Solutions do?

Elite Estimating Solutions prepares construction cost estimates and quantity takeoffs for contractors. You send your plans, and our trade estimators measure every quantity, price materials and labor for your project's ZIP code, and send back a detailed, bid ready estimate.

How fast will I get my estimate?

Most estimates are delivered in 24 to 48 hours from when we receive your plans. Larger or more complex projects can take longer, and we tell you the delivery time before we start.

Which trades do you estimate?

We cover every major trade, including concrete, masonry, roofing, drywall, paint, flooring, plumbing, HVAC, mechanical, electrical, MEP and sitework, plus full quantity takeoffs for general contractors.

How is my estimate priced?

Labor and material costs are priced for the ZIP code of your project, so the numbers match the local market you are bidding in. Our fee is quoted per project, with no subscription or retainer.

What do I need to send you?

Send your plans or drawings, the trade divisions you need, and your bid date. If you do not have plans yet, describe the scope of work and we will tell you what we need.

Do you work in Canada?

Yes. We estimate projects for contractors across the United States and Canada.

Is there a discount for new clients?

Yes. Your first project with us is priced at 50% off our standard rate.

Is Elite Estimating Solutions the same company as Elite Estimation Services?

No. Elite Estimating Solutions is an independent company with its own team, based in Kalispell, Montana, and found at eliteestimatingsolutions.com. We are not connected to Elite Estimation Services or any other company with a similar name. To reach us, use the contact details on this website.
